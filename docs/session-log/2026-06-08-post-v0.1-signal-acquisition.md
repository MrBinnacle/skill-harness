## Method note (procedural learnings)

- **HALT-as-deliverable pattern surfaced**: the discipline catching its own
  inconsistency is more credible than the original deliverable it was catching.
  Extracted as quarantine skill (see Claudeception below).
- **Precondition-check pre-fire SOP gap surfaced and patched mid-session.** The
  PM-surfaced orchestrator error ("there should have been a step where someone
  on the council identified dependencies like this as part of SOP") drove a
  same-session SOP amendment. The discipline of disclosing the error in the
  commit body (precedent: Phase 3.4 M5 CARRYOVER) was applied.
- **Skin-in-the-game compounding**: PM is author of both the harness and the
  dogfooded skills. The case study's "uncomfortable demonstration on widely-used
  skills" framing is the author judging their own work through their own
  framework. Strongest available credibility signal. Should be made explicit
  in the rewrite, not soft-pedaled.
- **First-user vs world-pull distinction**: the signal-acquisition plan
  initially treated all "world-pull" as external/future. The PM's own continuous
  use of the artifact during the session WAS first-user signal that I was
  mis-categorizing as project-owner direction. Surfaced + reframed mid-session.
- **Sutherland reframe value**: a behavioral-economics frame produced
  substantively better marketing/positioning analysis than my default
  engineering-discipline frame. Worth invoking it explicitly when the question
  is "how will this artifact land on a reader" rather than "is the artifact
  correct."
