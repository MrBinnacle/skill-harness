# Pre-registration — evaluation-result representation adequacy

**Lodged:** 2026-08-23, before any comparator was read in depth.
**Status:** OPEN. No evidence has been gathered against this plan.

This document is a pre-registration. It records the research question, the hypotheses, the
method, and the falsification criteria **before** the landscape review runs. Its value depends
entirely on that ordering. A criterion weakened after evidence arrives must be struck and
replaced by a dated amendment below, never edited in place.

---

## Research question

> What properties must an evaluation-result representation satisfy to preserve the
> decision-relevant information and epistemic boundaries of its underlying evidence?

## Hypotheses

**H1 (primary).** A result representation is inadequate when it collapses two evidential states
that license materially different downstream actions.

**H2 (secondary).** A result representation is unnecessarily complex when it preserves
distinctions that do not change any legitimate downstream decision or epistemic constraint.

**H3 (tertiary, added 2026-08-23 — see "Additions" below).** A result representation is
insufficient when a distinction it preserves in the schema does not survive to the point of
decision. Preservation in the artifact and availability at the decision are different
properties.

## Method

Construct a decision/distinction matrix from real evaluation states. Test candidate
representations pairwise for safe versus unsafe state collapse. Then test candidate schemas for
both false merges and unnecessary distinctions.

## Critical test

For every proposed distinction, identify the downstream action or epistemic claim that would
become invalid if the distinction were removed.

## Additional requirement

Test not only whether the result is preserved, but whether attribution scope, experimental
limitations, and applicability conditions survive serialization into the machine-readable
representation.

## SERS status

SERS is the current candidate representation generated by the needs of `skill-harness`. It is
not the research conclusion. This research must be capable of demonstrating that SERS is
incomplete, unnecessarily complex, or inferior to an alternative.

SERS is a proposed reporting specification. The term "standard" is not used for it here.

## Additions lodged at pre-registration time

These three items were not in the question as first stated. They are recorded now, before
evidence, because each changes how the analysis must be conducted. Each is separable and may be
struck by the owner without disturbing the rest.

### A1 — H2 requires an instantiated decision, not a conceivable one

As stated, H2 turns on the word *legitimate*. Without a bound, any distinction can be defended
by proposing a hypothetical decision that would need it, so H2 could never be confirmed, only
deflected.

**Rule adopted:** to defend a distinction under H2, name (a) a specific consumer of the
representation, (b) the specific action it takes, and (c) a case in which the two states lead
that consumer to act differently. A distinction with no instantiated case is recorded as
`UNNECESSARY_ON_CURRENT_EVIDENCE`, not as justified.

### A2 — H1 and H2 do not carry the same evidential burden, and must not be scored alike

H1 is refutable by a single counterexample: exhibit one pair that collapses without changing any
action, and the claimed distinction for that pair falls.

H2 requires establishing a negative — that **no** legitimate decision distinguishes two states.
Decisions cannot be exhaustively enumerated, so H2 is never provable.

**Rule adopted:** H2 findings are recorded from a time-boxed adversarial attempt to name a
differing action, with the attempt's scope and duration stated. Failure to find one is recorded
as weak evidence of redundancy, never as proof. Applying an H1-strength standard to an H2 claim
is a methodological error, and any finding that does so is void.

### A3 — the reachability failure mode, with a live instance

A representation can carry both states and their distinction and still fail, if the distinction
does not reach the consumer's decision point.

**Instance, verified 2026-08-23 in this repository.** `docs/sers/sers.schema.json` defines
`unmeasured_sub_reason` as a closed eight-member vocabulary including both `underpowered` and
`budget_exhausted`. Those two license different actions: `budget_exhausted` means more budget
completes the measurement; `underpowered` means the effect may not be resolvable at any
attainable sample size. The CLI render path collapsed them, printing a budget-exhausted clause
as `UNMEASURED(underpowered)`, because it fell back to a single default when the runner supplied
no sub-reason. The schema was correct throughout. The decision surface was not.

This is the failure H3 names, found in the representation this research is evaluating. It is
recorded here as the motivating case, not as a finding about any comparator.

Fix and evidence: PR #277.

## Falsification criteria for SERS

Recorded before evidence. Any of the following, if established, counts against SERS as a
distinct contribution:

1. An existing representation already encodes the same evidential states with the same
   action-relevant separations.
2. The SERS-specific states map onto an existing representation's states without changing any
   downstream action — a safe collapse under H1's own test.
3. The distinctions serve only this implementation and do not generalize to evaluation problems
   outside skill evaluation.
4. Interchange through SERS adds no downstream capability that interchange through an existing
   representation lacks.
5. The vocabulary is specific to skill evaluation in a way that makes it unsuitable as a general
   evaluation-result representation.
6. An existing representation provides an equivalent or better abstraction over the same states.

## Exit conditions

Exactly one of the following is recorded as the finding, with its evidence:

- `FALSIFIED` — existing work provides substantially equivalent semantics.
- `DISTINCT` — SERS represents material information existing systems do not.
- `USEFUL` — the distinction changes downstream interpretation or interoperability.
- `UNRESOLVED` — the evidence remains insufficient.

`DISTINCT` and `USEFUL` are not collapsed. A representation can differ without that difference
being worth a separate specification.

## Known counterexamples at lodging time

Both are output of the EvalEval Coalition. Neither has been compared in depth; they are named
here so that no novelty claim can rest on their absence.

- *Every Eval Ever: A Unifying Schema and Community Repository for AI Evaluation Results* —
  arXiv 2606.14516, June 2026. Reports coverage of 22,235 models, 2,273 benchmarks, and 31
  evaluation formats, with converters from existing formats.
- *Evaluation Cards: An Interpretive Layer for AI Evaluation Reporting* — arXiv 2606.09809,
  9 June 2026. Reports deployment across 5,816 models, 635 benchmarks, and 101,843 results, with
  four interpretive signals: reproducibility, documentation completeness, provenance and risk,
  and score comparability.

These figures are as reported by those projects and have not been independently verified here.
