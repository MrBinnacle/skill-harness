#503: unify the refusal-reason vocabulary — runner literals become UnmeasuredSubReason members

Two runner literals (tier2_uncalibrated, length_confounded) were free-form
strings outside the declared UnmeasuredSubReason enumeration, so a reader
grouping refusals by sub-reason worked from two disjoint vocabularies.

Add two members to UnmeasuredSubReason and narrow the runner's
ClauseResult.unmeasured_reason from str | None to UnmeasuredSubReason | None:

  tier2_uncalibrated -> TIER2_UNCALIBRATED (BLOCKER-1: not Tier-1-measurable;
    distinct from MECHANICAL_VACUOUS, which names an axis-registry scoreability
    property and does not cover a tier-2 clause whose axis IS registered)
  length_confounded   -> LENGTH_CONFOUNDED (QUAL-1: operator out of tolerance;
    no existing member names an operator-tolerance refusal)

The enum is now the single source of truth for refusal reasons. Existing
string-equality assertions (result.unmeasured_reason == tier2_uncalibrated)
still hold because StrEnum compares by value.

Criterion 4 (literals map to named members) and criterion 2 (one vocabulary)
pinned by tests in tests/test_issue_503_unmeasured_reason.py.
