Self-hosted typefaces for the receipts site.

Each WOFF2 file here is the latin subset published by Fontsource, fetched from
https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/fontsource/fonts/ on 2026-09-16, and each ships beside
the SIL Open Font License 1.1 text that covers it.

The OFL texts come from google/fonts: ofl/archivo/OFL.txt, ofl/archivoblack/OFL.txt
and ofl/jetbrainsmono/OFL.txt. They are not byte-identical to upstream. This
repository's pre-commit hooks strip trailing whitespace and normalise line
endings, and they rewrote these files on the commit that added them. The licence
terms are unchanged; only whitespace is. No hash is recorded for them for that
reason, and the hashes above cover the binaries alone, which the hooks leave
untouched.
